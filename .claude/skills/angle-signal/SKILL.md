# Angle: signal

Our own edge: a narrative cracking or inflecting, read by our analysis. It is the angle that grows paid readers, because nobody else has it.

**Never a fair value** (Thierry 2026-11-16: « not confident in our fair values »): a post proves the narrative break with a REPORTED figure and the shortest convincing why.

## Where the input comes from
- `gold.content_ideas` (the day's Ideas, scored per format; content-ideas), each citing its sources: Signal (`gold.signals`), Signal Changes (`gold.signal_changes`), Price Drivers with their belief and reversal (`gold.kpi_drivers`), the events a reversal read (`gold.driver_events`).
- The writer gets, per Idea: the title, why now, the narrative, the signal side, the cited events, the Price Drivers with their reversal, and **Reported figures** (each Price Driver's own measured series with its periods) (`content-drafts/format.ts` `draftPrompt`). It never gets a fair value, so it never prints one.

## How to write it: 3 lines
**Every signal post, X and Substack, every tone, is these 3 lines in order, a blank line between them:**
1. **What the market believes**: the story the price tells, in plain words (never cash floor, EPS, guide), with the company and `$TICKER`, no figure in it (« SuperCom $SPCB: the market still prices a shrinking business. »).
2. **The real figure that contradicts it**: ONE reported figure (never two joined by « then » or « yet ») with its period (named as the picture labels it: Sep-26 is Q3, never Q2) and both values, a « record » included (the earlier value it beats) (« Q3 revenue $7.5M, up 21% from $6.2M a year earlier. »); a % never replaces the earlier value, and the picture is that quarterly figure's chart (Q3 revenue: quarterly bars, never FY bars); both values are printed numbers (a % alone fails) and cover the same kind of period, a quarter against a quarter, never against a full year. Copied digit for digit from one reversing driver's `Now:` line (with a frame drawn, from a headline the picture prints; a figure no headline prints fails): its measure, period as the picture boxes it, both values, its rounding, the words naming the second value's period or kind (« a year earlier », « in the June quarter », « expected »; « earlier », « before » or « record » alone fails); with a frame drawn, line 2 is ONE printed headline of a reversing block, copied as printed (a yearly figure or any figure no headline prints never leads): its measure, its numbers and its comparison word (« Q3 sales 5.0M misses 6.0M » gives « Q3 sales $5.0M, against $6.0M expected »). A headline stays as printed: one value or one range stays alone (« FY2026 profit HK$165M to HK$175M »; never an earlier value, an « against » clause, a second period or a %, in the text, the Article title or its subtitle); a measure no block prints never leads; line 1 is the belief of the same block, and the picture's side decides SELL (crack) or BUY (inflection). Line 1, line 3 and the emoji follow that block's side (inflection 📈, crack 📉). With no driver, from Reported figures or Evidence, of the measure the picture charts (a gross margin text needs the gross margin chart, a quarterly revenue text the quarterly revenue chart, never a growth-rate chart); the figure moves against line 1's belief (BUY: turning up, SELL: turning down) and the % comes from those two values.
3. **Why it breaks the story: one sentence naming one concrete event the company did or lost (a new contract, a lost customer, a price rise, a new product), no number; any trend in sales, revenue, growth or margin, and a missed forecast, are results, never a cause (« revenue growing again » fails): name one action with a verb and a named object (signed a contract with a US customer, launched a product), never sales, customers or demand (« sold more treatments », « drew more customers » are results); never a vague verb (expanded, spent, kept spending, built, ramped up, pitched), never something the company has NOT done (« signed no contract »), never a result (« earned less »); a filing, profit alert, forecast, announcement, insider purchase, dividend, share, note or debt sale, refinancing, or anything the company said is never line 3: name a commercial action (signed a contract, launched a product, lost a customer), and « no cause named » is never line 3** (« New contracts now make up most of sales. »); « sold more of its services » is a sales result and never line 3.
- Then « Source: Dualis Research » on its own line.
- **Layout**: urgent and balanced may open line 1 with 📈 (a story turning up, BUY side) or 📉 (a loved story cracking, SELL side); neutral uses spacing only. The tone changes the words, never the 3 lines.
- **Length**: X post at most 250 characters (the disclaimer follows, X stops at 280); Note at most 400 characters; an Article opens with the 3 lines as its first section, then gives the evidence.
- **Frame drawn**: read the picture first; every figure of the whole text, Article included, is one the picture prints, and line 2 is the printed REALITY headline of ONE block, copied word for word, adding no word of its own (no « operating », quarter name, period or second value), never a fiscal-year or other figure the headline lacks (jargon in plain words, « vs » written « against »): « Quarterly margin 0.6% vs 38.6% » gives « Quarterly margin 0.6%, against 38.6% », with no « operating », no quarter name, no period and no clause the headline lacks; the text's side, measure and number are that block's, never a figure from other inputs. Line 1 is that block's belief, line 3 one company action behind it, and the side is the frame's: an event the frame omits (a contract, a purchase) never leads. Every figure of the whole text is printed on the picture. Line 2 is a printed headline only: pick the block first, then write; a measure with no block (margin, cash) never appears anywhere in the text, nor a figure the picture lacks (a yearly total). **Only with no frame drawn AND no driver or reported figure**: line 2 states the dated fact in words, no number, never the share price. With a frame drawn, every post of the company (Article included) takes its side, line 1 and line 2 from ONE printed block, line 2 being that headline word for word; a profit, margin, cash or forecast figure the picture does not print never appears, even when the inputs list it.
- The picture: skill `content-matrix`, « The picture » (the chart of the line-2 figure, both periods boxed).
- Say « profit for shareholders », never « net income ». One measure across the 3 lines. Line 2 always prints two values with their periods, a negative or low reading included (« margin -15.3%, against 3.9% in the June quarter »), except when a frame is drawn: then it is the printed headline exactly, one value stays one value.

## A post with no BUY or SELL (a weak signal)
Still the 3 lines above and the Source line, on ONE measure: the line-2 figure with its period and both values, the same measure in every line, no second result, its own chart. The last line is « Source: Dualis Research ».

## Never
- A fair value, a gap to it (« 24% under fair value »), a range, an upside or downside %, a price target, a horizon or the word « opportunity ».
- A figure absent from the inputs, or a % change without both of its values.
- Our internal words (« driver », « evidence », « thesis state », « signal strength », « gold ») in the post.
